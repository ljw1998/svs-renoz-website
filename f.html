    How we work

    From first visit to handover, in seven steps

    These are the same stages our team tracks for every project, so you always know where yours stands.

    01

    Free consultation

    Meet your Home Style Consultant at the showroom or your home. Tell us how you live and what you want to change.

    Site measurement & design

    We measure the unit, plan the layout and prepare design drawings for your review.

    Quotation

    An itemised quotation, room by room, from a package or fully customised.

    Confirmation letter

    Once you agree, we issue a confirmation letter with the final scope and payment stages.

    Pre-construction & materials

    Working drawings, management approval support and material ordering.

    Construction

    Our site team works through each trade on a set schedule, with progress updates along the way.

    Defect check & handover

    We check every item with you, clean the unit and hand over the keys.

    A typical site schedule

    Our default plan for a full condominium renovation runs about 50 days on site. Trades overlap where it is safe to. Your schedule depends on your scope and building rules.
