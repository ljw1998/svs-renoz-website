How we work

From first visit to handover, in seven steps

These are the same stages our team tracks for every project, so you always know where yours stands.

  1. Free consultation

    Meet your Home Style Consultant at the showroom or your home. Tell us how you live and what you want to change.

  2. Site measurement & design

    We measure the unit, plan the layout and prepare design drawings for your review.

  3. Quotation

    An itemised quotation, room by room, from a package or fully customised.

  4. Confirmation letter

    Once you agree, we issue a confirmation letter with the final scope and payment stages.

  5. Pre-construction & materials

    Working drawings, management approval support and material ordering.

  6. Construction

    Our site team works through each trade on a set schedule, with progress updates along the way.

  7. Defect check & handover

    We check every item with you, clean the unit and hand over the keys.

A typical site schedule

Our default plan for a full condominium renovation runs about 50 days on site. Trades overlap where it is safe to. Your schedule depends on your scope and building rules.

FAQ

Questions homeowners ask us

Is the first consultation really free?

Yes. Your first meeting with a Home Style Consultant costs nothing and does not commit you to anything.

How long does a renovation take?

A full condominium renovation usually takes about 50 days on site after materials are ready. Smaller jobs such as a kitchen or wardrobes take less. We confirm your schedule in writing.

Do you work on landed homes as well as condos?

Yes. We work on condominiums, apartments, terrace houses, semi-Ds and bungalows, and on some commercial spaces.

Should I choose a package or a custom design?

Packages suit common layouts and help you decide faster. Custom design suits special layouts or tastes. Your consultant can show you both, and you can add extras to a package.

How do payments work?

Payments are split into stages that follow the work. The stages and amounts are written in your confirmation letter before anything starts.

What if I want to change something after signing?

Tell your consultant. Changes are written up as a variation order for you to approve, so the scope and price stay clear.
